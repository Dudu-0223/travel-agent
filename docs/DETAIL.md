# Travel agent

Open **Travel agent** in the left sidebar. Each card is one trip; type where and when you want to go, and a new card appears while the assistant researches flights in the background on Trip.com, Ctrip, Fliggy, Google Flights and Skyscanner. It only researches; it never books.

Open a card to see its **Flights** view: the holidays it assumed, which sites it searched, and the recommended options. Options are ranked in tiers by price, leave days and transfers, and the first tier carries the cheapest, least-leave and easiest tags. Each option shows when you leave and when you get home; open it for the full schedule, transfers, leave dates and price links. Keep talking with the assistant below; when it needs a choice it asks with a question card.
